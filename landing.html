Frequently asked questions

What are GEX levels?

GEX (gamma exposure) shows how much market makers need to buy or sell as price moves, calculated from the options market. Where the exposure is large, levels form that price often reacts to: the Call Wall acts as a ceiling, the Put Wall as a floor, and the Gamma Flip separates a calm market from one that amplifies moves.
